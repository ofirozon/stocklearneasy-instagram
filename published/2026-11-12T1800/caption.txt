Why pay $200 for $1 of profit?

P/E Ratio

P/E RATIO
In March 2000, Cisco's P/E passed 200: you paid $200 for every $1 it earned a year, betting on growth. By 2002 the stock had fallen about 90%.

Before you buy, ask what you're paying for each $1 of profit.

Have you ever paid way more for something because of what it might become?
