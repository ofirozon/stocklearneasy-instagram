Bear Market

BEAR MARKET
A 20% fall from the top is a bear market. US stocks have had 27 since 1928, lasting about 10 months on average.

One comes every few years, so don't invest cash you'll need this year.

Did you ever watch something you owned lose a fifth of its value? What did you do?
