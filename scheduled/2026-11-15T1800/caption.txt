Can a $10 stock cost more than $200?

P/E Ratio

P/E RATIO
A $10 stock earning 20¢ a share has a P/E of 50, while a $200 stock earning $20 has a P/E of 10. Per dollar of profit, the $10 stock costs you 5x more.

Before calling a stock cheap, check its P/E against the S&P's long-run 16.

Which costs less per $1 of profit: P/E 40 at $5, or P/E 12 at $300?
