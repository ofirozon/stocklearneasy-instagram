Your Social Security raise could quietly shrink

Is it curtains for COLA? Saving Social Security could mean changing the cost-of-living adjustment.

INFLATION-ADJUSTED INCOME
Since 1975, Social Security checks rise each year with an inflation gauge, CPI-W. That's how they jumped 8.7% in 2023, so a slower gauge means smaller raises.

A raise that trails prices is a pay cut in slow motion.

Would you take smaller yearly raises or higher taxes to keep Social Security going?
