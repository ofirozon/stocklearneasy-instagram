## 2026-09-29 (evening): visual redesign in the app's own brand

## 2026-09-30: the words, and the white slab under them
- **Ofir's feedback, by voice, twice in five minutes.** First: the posts improved a lot and the image design now looks professional, what is left is the copy, "רואים מלל לפעמים ארוך מדי", and the slides do not flow into each other. Then, looking at a card: "רק הריבוע הלבן עם המלל בעייתי".
- **Changed, copy:** the `explain` cap went from 320 to 200 characters and `takeaway` from 90 to 80, both prompts gained a slide-flow block (the explain must answer the hook and not a neighbouring question, the takeaway lands rather than summarises, no slide re-introduces what the previous one just said, short sentences because the card is read at a glance), and the caps are now **enforced in `_clean_copy`** with `_COPY_LIMITS`. Until today they lived only in the prompt and nothing checked them, so an over-long paragraph shipped as smaller type via the fit script. Over the limit now means the copy is refused and asked for again once, and only then falls back to the template.
- **Changed, design:** `.lesson` was a solid `#f8fafc` card, taken from the app's white lesson list. On a navy post it read as a bright slab pasted over the design. It is now `rgba(255,255,255,0.055)` with a hairline border and light text, and it lost `flex:1`, so it hugs its copy instead of stretching to the footer. The concept block is centred now that the panel no longer fills the space. The reel picks up the same change, since `reel.py` uses the same class.
- **Applied to the queue:** four future slots rewritten by hand to the new limits (10-01 12:00 and 18:00, 10-02 12:00 and 18:00; 224 to 162, 271 to 174, 277 to 170, 255 to 188 characters) and re-rendered, both reels re-uploaded to the `media` release.
- **Aimed at:** a scroller who reads a card in about a second and a half. A 320-character paragraph in a white box is scrolled past, not read.
- **Numbers at the time:** unchanged from the previous entry, reach about 1 per post. This is a quality change, not a reach change, and it should not be credited with a reach move if one shows up.
- **Open at the end of the evening, waiting on Ofir.** He looked at the translucent panel and said it reads as a web component, not as an Instagram card ("השני בכחול יותר ווב"). A third version was rendered and sent to him: **no container at all**, the explain sits directly on the background at a larger size with a 5px green rule on its left. Concretely that is `.lesson` with `background:transparent; border:0; border-left:5px solid GREEN; border-radius:0`, the card's panel padding changed to `6px 0 6px 44px`, and the `explain` size tiers raised to 52/46/42. **Not applied.** The committed state is still the translucent panel. If he says yes, apply those three edits, rebuild the queued slots, re-upload the reels, and note it here.

## 2026-10-03 (night): the numbers came back, and they say reach is the wall
- **Instructed by Ofir, by voice at 21:33:** keep researching into the night, keep looking for what can be improved, run whatever is needed on the Mac, no purchases.
- **Insights work again.** The Keychain token was re-authorized earlier this evening and `ig-token-refresh.sh` now covers both accounts and syncs the GitHub secret. The 3.10 entry above says the numbers are unavailable; that is no longer true.
- **7 days, 13 posts: reach 28 total, views 70, likes 3, comments 0, saved 0, shares 0, followers 2.** Per post the reach is 1 to 5. The reels are not reaching further than the carousels: 3.10's 09:30 reel got reach 1.
- **What this means, stated plainly.** Content quality is no longer the binding constraint. At 2 followers and a reach of 1 to 5, no change to a card, a hook, a chart or a caption can be measured, because there is no sample. Every improvement logged above was worth making and none of them could have moved these numbers. The levers that do move cold-start reach are a human being present in the places the audience already is, collaborations, or paid promotion. Ofir has said he has no manual time for this account and does not want to buy promotion, which is a legitimate choice; it just means the honest forecast for this page is to stay near zero.
- **Shipped anyway, as the one improvement of the run:** the last slide asks for a send instead of a save ("RULE OF THUMB · SEND TO A FRIEND"). A send is the action Instagram says it weighs most when recommending to non-followers, and it is the only ask that moves a card to someone who has never heard of the account. Seven days of asking for a save produced zero saves. **Do not credit or blame this for a move in `shares`:** at this reach it is unmeasurable, and it is recorded here as a direction, not a result.
- **Two real defects found and fixed, both silent:**
  - The publisher sent every due slot in one pass. At 21:04 today the 09:30 reel, the 12:00 reel and the 18:00 carousel all went live inside two minutes. Now one slot per run with 45 minutes between publishes, and slots past `MAX_LATE_HOURS` move to `skipped/` instead of being re-skipped forever.
  - `generate.py rebuild` dropped `video_url` from `reel.json`, which demotes a reel slot to its carousel and a reel-only slot to nothing. It was repaired each morning by the queue script's re-upload, which is why nobody saw it. `build_reel` now uploads the video and records the URL itself.
- **Open, and worth Ofir's answer:** the account posts up to 4 times a day (two generated slots plus queued episodes). At reach 2 that is spraying; one strong post a day is the usual remedy, since per-post signal is what the ranker learns from. Asked him directly rather than changing his cadence unilaterally.
- **Noticed, not fixed:** three queued slots are reel-only with no `source.json` and no cards, so they cannot be rebuilt and have no carousel to fall back on if the reel fails.
