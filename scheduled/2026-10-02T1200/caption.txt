Borrowing just got pricier. Can a Hollywood mega-deal still get funded?

Hollywood’s big debt deal hits a wall of higher yields as Paramount finances Warner Bros. buyout

$BOWL.L

DEBT COSTS SHAPE DEALS
Paramount is borrowing heavily to buy Warner Bros., so it must sell bonds (loans from investors). With yields (interest investors demand) rising in 2026, every dollar borrowed costs more each year. If lenders still show up at those rates, it signals big-deal appetite is alive.

When yields rise, deals built on borrowed money get more expensive to pull off.
