Apple's iPhones aren't where the real money is

Apple’s iPhone business is booming. Here’s why the stock is falling anyway.

$AAPL

PROFIT MIX MATTERS
Apple keeps about 74 cents of every services dollar after costs, versus 37 cents on hardware (fiscal 2024). So a slowing services business outweighs booming iPhone sales.

Don't ask what sells most. Ask what keeps the most per dollar.
