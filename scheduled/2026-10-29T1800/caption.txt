Nvidia went from $1,200 to $120 overnight. Cheaper?

P/E Ratio

P/E RATIO
It split each share into 10, so profit per share split too. At $1,200 earning $20 a share, the P/E is 60; at $120 earning $2, it's still 60.

A low share price isn't a sale. Check the P/E before you call it cheap.
