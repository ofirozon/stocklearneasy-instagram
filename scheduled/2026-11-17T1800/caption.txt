What does $1 of profit actually cost you?

P/E Ratio

P/E RATIO
A $50 stock earning $2 a share has a P/E of 25: you pay $25 for each $1 of yearly profit. The S&P 500's long-run average is about 16.

Compare stocks by what $1 of profit costs, not by the share price.
