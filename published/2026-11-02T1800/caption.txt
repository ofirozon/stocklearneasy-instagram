Why is a $50 stock pricier than $500?

P/E Ratio

P/E RATIO
A $50 stock earning $2 a share has a P/E of 25. A $500 stock earning $50 has a P/E of 10, so you pay less per dollar of profit.

Before calling a stock cheap, ask what you pay per dollar of profit.
