How a great company becomes a bad buy

P/E Ratio

P/E RATIO
In 2000, Cisco hit $80 a share but earned about 40 cents. That P/E near 200 meant you paid for 200 years of its profit.

Before you buy, ask how many years of profit you're paying for.

How many years of its profit would you pay to buy a local coffee shop?
