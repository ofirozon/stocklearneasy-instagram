What is an ETF? 🧺

An ETF is a pooled fund that trades on an exchange like a stock.

Swipe to see what it can hold, how it trades, what it costs, and that it can still lose value.

Before you look at any ETF, ask three questions: What does it hold? What does it cost? How does it trade?
