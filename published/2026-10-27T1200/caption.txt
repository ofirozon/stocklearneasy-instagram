Your car may be your biggest money leak

Young Americans are giving up on car ownership. It’s a path to financial freedom for some.

OPPORTUNITY COST OF OWNERSHIP
Owning a new car cost over $12,000 a year in 2023, per AAA. Going car-free turns that roughly $1,000 a month into money you can save or spend.

The fastest raise is often a bill you stop paying.
