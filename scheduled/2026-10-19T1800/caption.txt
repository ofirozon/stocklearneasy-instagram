Why can't you buy a private company's stock?

IPO

IPO
A private company's shares aren't for sale to you. Say it sells 10 million shares to the public at $20 each, raising $200 million, and then anyone can trade them.

You won't usually get the $20 price, so check what you'd really pay.

If shares sold at $20 and opened at $30, did the $20 buyers gain or lose?
