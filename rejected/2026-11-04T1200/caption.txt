Your parents' $10,000 now beats $100,000 later

Want to help your adult kids and grandkids? Give them money while you’re still alive.

COMPOUNDING NEEDS TIME
At the S&P 500's long-run average of about 10% a year, $10,000 given at 25 grows to roughly $450,000 by 65. A $100,000 inheritance at 65 gets none of those 40 years.

Money you get at 25 does way more work than money you get at 65.

Would you rather get $10,000 from family now or $100,000 when you're 65?
