Great earnings can still lose to higher rates

Stocks are heading for a high-stakes showdown, as strong earnings collide with rising borrowing costs

RISING RATES SHRINK VALUATIONS
In 2022 the 10-year Treasury yield (US borrowing cost) rose from 1.5% to 3.9%. The Nasdaq fell 33% while Apple posted a record $99.8 billion profit.

When safe bonds pay more, future profits are worth less today.

If a safe bond paid you 4%, would you still hold a risky tech stock?
