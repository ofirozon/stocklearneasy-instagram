Why do high rates make retirement paychecks bigger?

The hidden silver lining of high interest rates: safer, cheaper retirement income

RATES SET FIXED PAYOUTS
An annuity (lifetime paycheck plan) puts your lump sum into bonds. When bonds pay more interest, the insurer can promise you a bigger monthly check.

When rates rise, each saved dollar buys more future income.

Would you swap a lump sum for a lifetime monthly check at today's rates?
