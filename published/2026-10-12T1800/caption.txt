Fed’s Powell says rate cut ‘on the table’ as soon as September

HOW RATE CUTS SPREAD
The Fed sets the rate banks pay to borrow overnight. It votes on that rate 8 times a year, and a cut ripples into your card and car loan rates.

When the Fed cuts, almost everyone's borrowing gets a little cheaper.

If your loan payment dropped, would you pay it off faster or spend the extra?
