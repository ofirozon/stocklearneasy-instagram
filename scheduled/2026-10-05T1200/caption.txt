Your pay rose 5%. So why could you buy less?

Falling wages, soaring energy prices and inflation: It’s beginning to look a lot like the 1970s

REAL VERSUS NOMINAL MONEY
In 1973 oil jumped from $3 to $12 a barrel, lifting prices on almost everything. Prices rose about 10% a year, so a 5% raise bought less: real pay, what money buys, shrank.

Judge any gain by what it buys, not by the number.
