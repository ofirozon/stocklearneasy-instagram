Last time this mix hit, stocks nearly halved

Falling wages, soaring energy prices and inflation: It’s beginning to look a lot like the 1970s

STAGFLATION HITS STOCKS
In 1973 an oil embargo quadrupled oil prices while pay fell behind. The S&P 500, the 500 biggest US companies, dropped about 48% by late 1974.

When everything costs more, companies earn less, and stocks feel it.
