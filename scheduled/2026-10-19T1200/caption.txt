Inflation ran hot. Bond buyers bought anyway.

The bond market shrugged off two inflation shocks. Why stock investors should pay attention.

WHEN YIELDS GET ATTRACTIVE
Two hot price reports usually push yields (what bonds pay) up. Buyers decided yields were high enough to lock in, like when the 10-year hit 5% in Oct 2023, a 16-year high.

Once bonds pay enough, people buy them even on bad news.
