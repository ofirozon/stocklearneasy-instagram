SpaceX's $40B loan could cost $5M a day

Musk says SpaceX landed a $40 billion debt deal — and forecasts doom for some AI rivals

DEBT COMES BEFORE PROFIT
At a 5% rate, $40 billion means $2 billion a year in interest, roughly $5.5 million every day. The chip rentals have to earn that before any profit counts.

Lenders get paid first, owners only get what's left.

Would you rent out chips on borrowed money, or wait until you had the cash?
