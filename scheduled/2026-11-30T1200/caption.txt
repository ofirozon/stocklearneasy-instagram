All at once, or a little each month? 🪙

Dollar-cost averaging means investing equal amounts at regular intervals. Swipe through two made-up price examples: in one, buying monthly ends with slightly more shares. In the other, buying all at once ends with more.

Takeaway: spreading purchases changes the price you pay. It does not promise a better result.
