Would you pay 25 years of profit upfront?

P/E Ratio

P/E RATIO
A $50 stock earning $2 a share has a P/E of 25: you pay for 25 years of today's profit. The S&P 500 has averaged about 16.

Before you buy, ask how many years of profit you're paying for.

Have you bought something that took years to pay for itself, like a car or a degree?
