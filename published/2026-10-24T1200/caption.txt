Your newborn's free $1,000 isn't automatic

The Social Security Administration wants more babies signed up for ‘Trump accounts.’ But there’s still a catch.

COMPOUND GROWTH OVER TIME
Babies born 2025 to 2028 get $1,000 from the Treasury, but a parent must claim it on IRS Form 4547. At 7% a year, that's about $3,400 by age 18.

Free money only compounds once someone actually signs the form.

Would you have known to sign your baby up, or assumed it was automatic?
