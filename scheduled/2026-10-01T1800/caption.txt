Anthropic’s potential $2 trillion IPO comes with the following fine print

$ANTW

REVENUE GROWTH VS. MARGINS
Anthropic's revenue grew more than 10x in 2025. But every Claude answer runs on expensive chips, and training each new model costs even more. If costs rise as fast as sales, profit (money left over) barely moves. That's the catch behind a potential $2 trillion price tag.

Sales growth only builds profit when costs grow slower than revenue.

What matters more for Anthropic's IPO: 10x sales growth or its rising model costs?
