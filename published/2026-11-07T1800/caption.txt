McDonald’s tests new value menu as fast-food discount wars heat up

VOLUME OVER MARGIN
A lower price earns less per burger but pulls more people through the door. McDonald's leaned on this in 2002 with its $1 Dollar Menu, betting extra visits beat thinner margins.

A discount only pays if enough extra people show up.
