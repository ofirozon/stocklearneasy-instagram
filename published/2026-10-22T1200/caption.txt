Playing it safe now costs more than tech

Investors’ search for safety has made toothpaste stocks more expensive than tech

PAYING UP FOR SAFETY
US stocks have averaged about $16 of price per $1 of yearly profit. Fear pushed toothpaste makers past tech, so you pay growth prices for a product nobody buys more of in a boom.

Safety stops being cheap once everyone runs to it at once.
