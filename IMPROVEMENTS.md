## 2026-10-04 (evening): the rules left the prompts and became gates
- **Ofir, by voice, twice:** the posts look like an AI tool that does not know what it is doing wrote them, and separately, "למה אני כל פעם צריך לתזכר אותך לבדוק מהם המשימות שאני קובע לך". - **What was actually wrong, and it was worse than the posts.** A 244-line change to `generate.py` implementing backlog items 24 to 38 had been sitting uncommitted since the night of 3.10, along with an untracked `copy_check.py`. Nothing had run under it. Every post he was looking at was written under the old rules. The work existed and was not shipped, which is the failure mode that looks exactly like no work at all.
- **Shipped:** `copy_check.py` runs after the model answers, and a miss costs a retry whose prompt names the actual failure. Hard gates: 30 banned phrases including the hedges, a real quantity in the explain, no two slides opening on the same meaningful word, no hook reusing one of the last three openings, no second idea stapled on with "and also". Soft gate: Flesch-Kincaid above grade 9, reported every attempt and enforced on all but the last, because the fallback is the template copy. `generate.py` also checks the last 14 posts for topic overlap on two distinctive shared words before picking a headline, and stamps `backlog_items` and `copy_prompt` into every `source.json`.
- **Two defects found in that uncommitted work before it shipped.** The quantity gate accepted spelled counting words, so "the same three forces are lining up again" passed a gate whose entire purpose was to force a figure onto the card; it now needs a digit or a true magnitude word. And the fact rule, as drafted, banned every detail not present in the headline, which is correct for claims about the story and wrong for settled background facts. It made the explain worse in a measurable way: the old copy said oil went from $3 to $12 in 1973, the new copy said "the same three forces". The rule now separates the two, and the regenerated post reads "In 1973 an oil embargo quadrupled oil prices while pay fell behind. The S&P 500, the 500 biggest US companies, dropped about 48% by late 1974."
- **Backlog: 29 of 100 done.** Each ✅ now says whether the rule is gated in code or only present in a prompt, because a rule that is only asked for ships its exceptions. Items 2, 10 and 82 plus the 🔒 items are blocked on Ofir.
- **Process, changed so he does not have to chase it:** the improvement loop runs daily instead of twice a week, takes as many unblocked items as it can ship and verify in one run instead of exactly one, and its report opens with the backlog count before anything else.
- **Numbers at the time:** 2 followers, 11 posts over 7 days, reach 33, views 65, 1 like, 0 saves, 0 shares, 0 comments. Unchanged in substance from 3.10. Nothing above should be credited with a move in these numbers; at this reach the copy gates are a quality floor, not a growth lever.
