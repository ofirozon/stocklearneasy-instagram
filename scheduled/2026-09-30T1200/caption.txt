Hibbett still made sales. So why did the stock drop?

Hibbett shares fall as Q1 sales miss analysts’ estimate

EXPECTATIONS DRIVE PRICE MOVES
Hibbett, a sneaker and sportswear retailer, reported first-quarter sales below what analysts (professional Wall Street forecasters) predicted. The share price already assumed the forecast would be met, so falling short made the stock drop.

A stock reacts to results versus expectations, not to the results alone.
