📘 Term of the Day

Two stocks both end the week at $50. Why does one feel scarier?

Volatility

VOLATILITY
Stock A: $50, $51, $49, $50. Stock B: $50, $58, $43, $50. Same finish, but B jumped 16% one day and fell 26% the next. Those bigger, faster swings are volatility.

It tells you how bumpy the ride is, not whether the price ends up or down.

A stock goes $100, then $90, then $112 in 3 days. High or low volatility?
