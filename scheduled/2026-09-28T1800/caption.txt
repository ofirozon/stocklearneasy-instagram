Eggs hit $6. Did your paycheck grow that fast?

From $6 eggs to $50,000 cars, these charts show how inflation has defined the past 5 years

PURCHASING POWER EROSION
Inflation means prices rising across the economy. Over five years it pushed eggs to $6 and new cars past $50,000, so each dollar buys less than it used to. That squeeze is why consumer confidence, meaning how upbeat shoppers feel, has stayed low.

If prices rise faster than your money grows, you are quietly getting poorer.
