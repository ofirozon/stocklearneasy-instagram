Why a bull, and why a bear?

Two animals run the language of the stock market, and almost nobody explains
where they came from. A bull throws its horns upward. A bear swipes its paw
down. That is the whole difference, and it is why a rising market is bullish
and a falling one is bearish.

Save this one. It is the vocabulary every other market story is written in.
