DIVERSIFICATION THROUGH ETFS
An ETF (a basket fund) is one share that holds many companies at once. An S&P 500 fund, for example, owns about 500 U.S. firms. A sports bet is settled in one night, win or lose. Gen Z is starting young, so even small amounts get more years to compound (earn gains on gains).

Time in the market does more for small amounts than any single lucky bet.
