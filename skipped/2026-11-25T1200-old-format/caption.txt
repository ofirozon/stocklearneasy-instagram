Why pay $50 for $2 of profit?

P/E Ratio

P/E RATIO
A $50 stock earning $2 a share has a P/E of 25. At today's profit, that's 25 years to earn back your $50. US stocks have averaged about 16.

Don't call a stock cheap until you know how many years of profit it costs.

Have you bought something that saves you $20 a year? If it cost $200, what's its P/E?
