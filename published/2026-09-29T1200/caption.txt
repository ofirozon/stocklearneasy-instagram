📘 Term of the Day

Everyone says "bear market." But down how much, exactly?

Bear Market

BEAR MARKET
Say an index peaks at 5,000 points and slides to 4,000. That's a 20% drop from the high, the usual line for a bear market. A dip to 4,500 is only 10%, which is usually called a correction instead.

It measures how far prices fell from the peak, not where they go next.

A stock falls from $60 to $45. Is that a bear market-sized drop?
