# When to stop (backlog item 92)

Written 28.10.2026, before the numbers can argue for either side. The account
exists to drive installs of Stock Learn Easy, so the test is installs, not
followers. Stopping is Ofir's call; this file only says when the improve run
must put the question to him instead of shipping another tweak.

**Next check: 2027-01-28.** Then every three months. The improve run reads this
date; on or after it, it runs the check below and puts the result in its report.

## The check, over the 90 days before the check date

Raise "stop or change course" with Ofir if ALL three hold:

1. Fewer than 25 followers (3 on 28.10.2026).
2. Median reach per post under 20 over the last 30 days (2 for carousels, 5 for
   reels on 28.10.2026), from metrics/winners.md.
3. Fewer than 3 first-time downloads tagged "Web referrer" or "App referrer"
   in metrics/downloads.md across the 90 days (1 in 24.9 to 26.10.2026). Search
   and browse downloads do not count: Instagram cannot have caused them.

If only 1 and 2 hold but 3 does not, the account is small but converting; keep it.
If 3 holds but 1 and 2 do not, the account grows but does not sell; the next
quarter's work is the conversion tier (E), not reach.

## What "change course" means

Not deleting anything. The cheapest options, in order: cut to one post a day
(item 2), reels only (they reach 2 to 3 times what carousels do), or stop
generating and let the account sit. The pipeline costs Mac time and Claude
runs, not Ofir's time, so the bar to keep it is low; the bar to keep adding
backlog work to it is what this check is for.
