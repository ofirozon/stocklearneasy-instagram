49.4: why a score just under 50 has economists paying attention

Germany flash services PMI falls to 9-month low of 49.4

THE PMI 50 LINE
A PMI asks firms whether business grew, and 50 is the line between growing and shrinking. Germany's services, its banks, shops and hotels, just posted their weakest reading since December.

One month under 50 is a wobble. A run of them is a direction.

Services are most of Germany's economy. Does 49.4 worry you more than a factory miss?
