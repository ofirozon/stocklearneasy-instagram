📘 Term of the Day

Why not put all your money in your single best stock idea?

Diversification

DIVERSIFICATION
Put $1,000 in one stock and it drops 50%: you have $500 left. Split it across 10 stocks and the same drop hits one: you have $950.

Diversifying won't stop losses. It caps what one mistake can cost.

If all 10 of your stocks are tech companies, how diversified are you really?
