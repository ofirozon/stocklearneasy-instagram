Is that big price jump real or noise?

Volume

VOLUME
A $40 stock usually trades 1 million shares a day. If it jumps 5% on 8 million shares, a crowd moved it, not a handful of buyers.

Check the volume before you trust any big price move.

A price jumps 10% but only 50 shares traded all day. Would you trust that move?
