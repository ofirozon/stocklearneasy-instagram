How do you own 500 companies with just $100?

ETF

ETF
Put $100 into an S&P 500 ETF and you own a sliver of all 500 companies in one trade. If Apple is 7% of the fund, about $7 of your $100 rides on Apple.

One ticker, hundreds of companies: you buy the whole basket at once.

If one stock in a 500-stock ETF drops 20%, does your ETF drop 20%? Why not?
