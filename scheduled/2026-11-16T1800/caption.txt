Chinese stocks are jumping on one syllable: yang

China’s hottest stocks these days have one thing in common: names that sound like sheep

NARRATIVE OVER FUNDAMENTALS
Yang means sheep in Mandarin, and the Sheep year comes round once every 12 years. So fish and lamp makers with yang in their names rallied on the name, not on sales.

A price can rise on a joke. Check what the company actually sells.
