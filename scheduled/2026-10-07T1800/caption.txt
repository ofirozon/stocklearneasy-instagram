Short Selling

SHORT SELLING
You borrow 10 shares at $50 and sell them for $500. If the price drops to $30, buying them back costs $300 and you keep $200.

Before a short, ask how high it could climb, not how low.

You sold a borrowed $50 share. It climbs to $80. What's your loss?
