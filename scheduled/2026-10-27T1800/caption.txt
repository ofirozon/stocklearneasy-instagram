Market Order vs. Limit Order

MARKET ORDER VS. LIMIT ORDER
On May 6, 2010, Accenture plunged from $40 to $0.01 in minutes. Market sell orders filled at a penny, while a $39 limit order just waited.

Name your price with a limit, or the market names it for you.

Would you rather sell right now at any price, or wait for the price you want?
