A $7,000 share can be the cheap one

P/E Ratio

P/E RATIO
NVR costs about $7,500 a share and earns about $500, a P/E near 15. Tesla, at a fraction of that price, has a P/E above 100: over 6x more per $1 of profit.

Compare stocks by P/E, never by the share price tag.

Have you ever picked the cheaper item, then found it cost more per use?
