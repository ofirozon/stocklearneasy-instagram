Your loan rate follows this number down

Treasury yields log biggest drop since the 2023 banking crisis. Here’s what’s going on.

YIELDS SET BORROWING COSTS
Lenders price mortgages and car loans off Treasury yields, the US's borrowing rate. After SVB failed in March 2023, the 2-year yield fell about a full point in 3 days.

When yields fall, your loan gets cheaper and your savings pay less.
