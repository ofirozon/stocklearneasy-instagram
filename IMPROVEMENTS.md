# Stock Learn Easy IG: improvement log

One improvement per run of `stocklearneasy-ig-improve`. Read before choosing.

## 2026-09-29: the app finally gets named
- **Changed:** every caption now carries one line above the disclaimer ("📲 Want the full lesson? Stock Learn Easy on the App Store, link in bio."), and the reel's closing scene says "Daily lessons in the app. Link in bio." instead of "Follow for one story a day". `generate.py rebuild` now rewrites caption.txt too, so the 5 already-queued slots got the new line.
- **Aimed at:** installs. Before this, no post or reel mentioned the app anywhere; the only path was the bio link (which does point at the App Store, id6761296421).
- **Numbers at the time (7d):** 2 followers, 13 posts, reach 14 total (1 per post), views 44, likes 8, saves 0, shares 0. First reel (29.9 12:00 UTC) published OK: reach 1, views 4 after ~3h.
- **Real bottleneck:** reach, not conversion. Reach of 1 per post means Instagram shows it to almost nobody outside the account. Next runs should target non-follower reach (reel hook / first 2 seconds, reel vs carousel comparison once there are a few reels).

## 2026-09-29 (evening): visual redesign in the app's own brand
- **Changed:** cards and reel frames rebuilt on one shared look (`design.py`), picked from three directions on the design canvas "Stock Learn Easy IG Card Directions" (https://claude.ai/artifact/QrBJ9cWfd2hzq5CHMZHgY3, A. Lesson Card). Fonts are the app's own (DM Serif Display headings, DM Sans text) and load from `assets/fonts/` on disk: before this the Inter @import usually lost the race with the screenshot, so most cards went out in Helvetica. App icon in the header of every card; the numbers in a hook ("10x", "$50", "4-month") in the app's yellow; the lesson set in a light card like the app's lesson list; last slide is "Rule of thumb, save this" plus an app strip with the icon ("The full lesson is in the app"); progress dots instead of "2/4"; reel closes on the icon and "Investing, explained from zero." Chart: dashed line at the starting value, endpoint no longer cut by the edge. A fit script shrinks any unusually long text instead of letting it overflow, and Chrome now waits (`--virtual-time-budget`) for fonts before the shot.
- **Fixed on the way:** the 1.10 18:00 IPO post charted `$ANTW`, an "Anthropic AI Lab Ecosystem ETF", as if it were Anthropic. `lookup_ticker` now skips funds/ETFs/trusts, that slot's ticker was cleared (now 3 slides, no chart), and a chart with less history than its range says "since Aug 13" instead of "past 6 months".
- **Aimed at:** saves (explicit "save this" rule-of-thumb card) and installs (brand continuity from post to App Store page, icon on every card). Also plain quality: a scroller now sees a finance brand, not a template.
- **Numbers at the time:** unchanged from the entry above (reach ~1 per post). All 4 queued slots re-rendered, both reels re-uploaded.

## 2026-09-30: the words, and the white slab under them
- **Ofir's feedback, by voice, twice in five minutes.** First: the posts improved a lot and the image design now looks professional, what is left is the copy, "רואים מלל לפעמים ארוך מדי", and the slides do not flow into each other. Then, looking at a card: "רק הריבוע הלבן עם המלל בעייתי".
- **Changed, copy:** the `explain` cap went from 320 to 200 characters and `takeaway` from 90 to 80, both prompts gained a slide-flow block (the explain must answer the hook and not a neighbouring question, the takeaway lands rather than summarises, no slide re-introduces what the previous one just said, short sentences because the card is read at a glance), and the caps are now **enforced in `_clean_copy`** with `_COPY_LIMITS`. Until today they lived only in the prompt and nothing checked them, so an over-long paragraph shipped as smaller type via the fit script. Over the limit now means the copy is refused and asked for again once, and only then falls back to the template.
- **Changed, design:** `.lesson` was a solid `#f8fafc` card, taken from the app's white lesson list. On a navy post it read as a bright slab pasted over the design. It is now `rgba(255,255,255,0.055)` with a hairline border and light text, and it lost `flex:1`, so it hugs its copy instead of stretching to the footer. The concept block is centred now that the panel no longer fills the space. The reel picks up the same change, since `reel.py` uses the same class.
- **Applied to the queue:** four future slots rewritten by hand to the new limits (10-01 12:00 and 18:00, 10-02 12:00 and 18:00; 224 to 162, 271 to 174, 277 to 170, 255 to 188 characters) and re-rendered, both reels re-uploaded to the `media` release.
- **Aimed at:** a scroller who reads a card in about a second and a half. A 320-character paragraph in a white box is scrolled past, not read.
- **Numbers at the time:** unchanged from the previous entry, reach about 1 per post. This is a quality change, not a reach change, and it should not be credited with a reach move if one shows up.
- **Open at the end of the evening, waiting on Ofir.** He looked at the translucent panel and said it reads as a web component, not as an Instagram card ("השני בכחול יותר ווב"). A third version was rendered and sent to him: **no container at all**, the explain sits directly on the background at a larger size with a 5px green rule on its left. Concretely that is `.lesson` with `background:transparent; border:0; border-left:5px solid GREEN; border-radius:0`, the card's panel padding changed to `6px 0 6px 44px`, and the `explain` size tiers raised to 52/46/42. **Not applied.** The committed state is still the translucent panel. If he says yes, apply those three edits, rebuild the queued slots, re-upload the reels, and note it here.
- **Closed, 30.9 evening: the panel stays.** Shown the frameless version he chose the framed one ("עדיף עם המסגרת יותר יפה"). So the translucent panel with the hairline border is the final state, and it is what is already committed and queued. Do not re-open this without him asking.
