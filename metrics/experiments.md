# Running experiments and what would count as a win (backlog item 85)

Written 26.10.2026, before any of these has 10 posts. The point is to fix the
bar now, so a later run cannot look at the numbers first and then decide what
"better" meant. Read with winners.md, which has the medians.

Common rule for every line below: nothing is read before the variant has 10
posts (item 86), and the comparison is against the median of its parent shape
over the same weeks, not against the account's all-time median. A variant that
has not reached its bar after 10 posts is a loss and is switched off; one that
reaches it is kept, and the next experiment stacks on it.

Baselines on 26.10.2026: carousel median reach 2 (38 posts), news reel 5 (23).

At current reach (1 to 15 per post) a "2x" on a median of 2 is 4, which noise
can produce. Treat a win at this scale as "keep running", not as proof, and
re-read when the median is above 20.
